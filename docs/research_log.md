# Pre-registered research history

This record distinguishes the sequence of frozen tests from the unified-branch reporting layer. Effects below are from the archived aggregate reports; they are not re-estimated here.

## v1 — eight primary tests

Four hypotheses were tested at 21 and 63 sessions: Cohen–Malloy–Pomorski opportunistic versus routine-only purchases, the same comparison for sales, Hong–Li-style seller silence, and purchases following a market-adjusted decline of at least 30%. None passed the pre-registered joint criteria.

The buys-after-a-drop result was suggestive but unstable: at 63 sessions the ≥30% decline arm exceeded other purchases by **+3.14 pp**, 95% CI **[−1.11,+7.39]**, raw p=.147. The chronological halves were approximately **−1.2 pp** and **+6.3 pp**. Sensitivity thresholds were also null: at −20%, the 63-session difference was +1.14 pp (CI [−2.45,+4.72]); at −40%, +0.87 pp (CI [−4.15,+5.89]). The 21-session results were negative at all three thresholds.

## v2 — four primary tests

Open-market-only purchases were tested against zero and against financing-participation purchases at 21 and 63 sessions. None of the four tests passed. Financing participation performed at least as well in several comparisons, so the later purchase baseline did not exclude it.

## v3 — two registered holdout tests

Two 2026H1 tests (all open-market purchases and a frozen small-cap subset) were registered but remained pending because the required 2026 price bridge was unavailable. They are included in the archived count of 19 registered confirmatory tests but have no verdict.

## v4 — attention and size

H9, low minus high turnover within size terciles, failed: +0.237 pp at five sessions, CI [−0.703,+1.177], Holm-2 p=.310, with a negative 2020–2025 half. H10, the pooled low-turnover purchase mean, passed its two-test family rule at +1.049 pp, CI [+0.230,+1.868], Holm-2 p=.012; however, its global 16-test Holm value was about .10. The fixed interpretation was size rather than attention.

## v5 — rebound control and chronological filter validation

H11 tested whether the small-cap purchase drift survives control for the prior five-session return. It passed: intercept **+1.385 pp**, clustered SE 0.352 pp, one-sided p=5.836e−05; the 2009–2019 and 2020–2025 intercepts were +1.639 and +1.185 pp. H11 survived a Holm correction across the archived 19-test family (approximately .001).

Separately, 89 filter combinations were searched only on 2009–2019 and frozen before the 2020–2025 holdout. Two, rather than the planned three, met the search threshold. Neither confirmed in the holdout: officer plus above-median dollars produced +1.64 pp (one-sided p=.052; Holm-3 p=.156), while price at least $5 produced −0.90 pp. These filters remain descriptive leads, not changes to S1.

## v6 — historical-universe replication

The frozen small-cap rule was rebuilt on the independently built historical biotech universe. H13 passed its own pre-registered rule: five-session excess return **+1.509 pp**, N=2,684, clustered SE 0.386 pp, t=3.91; the 2009–2019 and 2020–2025 means were +1.614 and +1.405 pp. Its rebound-controlled intercept was +1.509 pp (SE 0.374 pp).

The unified branch subsequently replaced the incomplete v6 price cache with complete official-universe pricing without changing the rule. That produces the current S1 headline (+1.459 pp, N=3,524) while the shared regression test continues to reproduce the frozen v6 numbers.

## Confirmatory accounting and conclusions

The archived through-v5 count is **19 registered confirmatory tests**: v1 8, v2 4, v3 2 pending, v4 2, and v5 1 plus 2 actually selected holdout specifications. H10 survived its local two-test family; H11 survived the global 19-test correction. The two v3 tests remain unevaluated, and all other executed v1–v5 tests failed their declared criteria. V6/H13 is a subsequent one-test replication and passed its own rule.

The defensible conclusion is therefore narrow: purchases in smaller biotech firms show a short-horizon drift that is not explained by a simple rebound control and replicates across the historical-universe construction. Routine/opportunistic status, seller silence, post-drop buying, open-market-only status, attention, and the searched filter combinations did not provide confirmed incremental signals.
